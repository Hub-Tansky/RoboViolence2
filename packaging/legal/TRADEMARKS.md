# Robo Violence 2: names and logos

"Robo Violence 2", "RV2" and the Robo Violence 2 logo are the names of a game by Piotr Pełzowski. The code is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later) and the art has its own licence (LICENSE-ART.txt); neither grants rights to the names or logo (GPLv3 section 7(e)).

You may, without asking:
- say truthfully that your package, server, mod, map, video or article contains, runs or is about Robo Violence 2;
- package an unmodified official release under its own name for a distribution or store.

Ask first before you:
- release a modified version (a fork) under the name Robo Violence 2 or a confusingly similar name: a fork must use a different name and logo;
- use the name or logo in a product name, domain name, company name or merchandise;
- suggest your server, mod or package is official.
